How to play

Say what nobody else says.

Each prompt asks you to name something: a bird, a country with exactly 5 letters, a cheese starting with G. Any real answer counts. The less obvious it is, the more it scores.

  1. Low Orbit10What everyone says first
  2. Moonshot25Common

  4. Deep Space70Takes some knowledge
  5. Far Out100Expert-level, still real

Voyage

Fuel burns every second. Valid answers refuel you, and rarer answers refuel more. Wrong answers cost 5 seconds. Valid answers in a row raise your multiplier up to ×2.

Daily Orbit

Six prompts, two answers each, no clock. Everyone gets the same prompts today, so compare scores with friends.

Party

Two to eight players share one phone. Each player answers in secret, then everyone sees the reveal together. Scoring works like Crew. No internet needed.

Crew

One player creates a room and shares the code or link. Everyone answers the same prompt at once. An answer only you gave earns a Lone Signal (+50%). If two players give the same answer, they collide and split the points. Answers missing from the word bank go to a quick crew vote.

Typos

Spell it how it sounds. "Brocoli" counts as Broccoli and "Phillipines" as the Philippines, and the game shows what it fixed. If a typo is close to two answers, it asks which one you meant, with no penalty. Plurals, capital letters and accents never matter.

How Far Out works

  1. You get a question, like “Name a red fruit”.
  2. Type any real answer. Typos are fine; the game fixes them or asks what you meant.
  3. Rarer answers fly farther. Strawberry is Low Orbit, 10 points. Lingonberry is Far Out, 100.
