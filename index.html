How to play

Say what nobody else says.

Every question asks you to name something: a red fruit, something you'd find in a garage, a Pixar film. Any real answer counts. The less obvious it is, the more it scores.

  1. Low Orbit10What everyone says first
  2. Moonshot25Common
  3. Red Planet45Known, not a first thought
  4. Deep Space70Takes some knowledge
  5. Far Out100Expert-level, still real

Daily Orbit

Six questions, two answers each, no clock. Everyone gets the same six today, so compare with friends. Finish every day to grow your streak.

Voyage

Fuel burns every second. Valid answers refuel you, and rarer answers refuel more. A wrong answer costs 3 seconds. Valid answers in a row raise your multiplier up to ×2.

Meteor Shower

One question, 60 seconds, as many answers as you can. Three answers in a row at Red Planet or rarer start Hyperdrive: double points for 10 seconds.

Spectrum

Find one answer for each of the five rarities on the same question, from Low Orbit to Far Out, within 90 seconds. Two hints per game.

Twin Stars

No typing. You see two answers to a question: tap the rarer one. Three lives, and the pairs get closer as you go.

Duel

Vega, the game's rival pilot, answers first. Name something rarer to win the round. First to three wins. Pick Easy, Normal or Hard.

Explore

No clock and no end. Answer, ask for a hint, skip, and learn the rare answers.

Party

Two to eight players share one phone. Each player answers in secret, then everyone sees the reveal together. An answer only you gave earns a bonus; the same answer as someone else splits the points. No internet needed.

Typos

Spell it how it sounds. "Brocoli" counts as Broccoli and "Phillipines" as the Philippines, and the game shows what it fixed. If a typo is close to two answers, it asks which one you meant, with no penalty. Plurals, capital letters and accents never matter.

Hebrew

Answer in English or in Hebrew: "בננה" counts as Banana, and Israeli answers like Reva LeSheva (רבע לשבע) count too. Typed with the keyboard in the wrong language? "ןאשךט" still counts as Italy.

Levels and badges

Every valid answer earns XP equal to its points, in every mode. Level up from Cadet to Far Out Legend, and collect badges in your logbook. It all stays in this browser.

How Far Out works

  1. You get a question, like “Name a red fruit”.
  2. Type any real answer. Typos are fine; the game fixes them or asks what you meant.
  3. Rarer answers fly farther. Strawberry is Low Orbit, 10 points. Lingonberry is Far Out, 100.
